# 🔬 Project Overview

Binary alloys consist of two principal elements and provide an opportunity to study the influence of composition and processing on microstructure and properties.

This project investigates binary Al-Zn alloys with different zinc contents fabricated through casting followed by solid-solution heat treatment.

The study focuses on the influence of Zn content and heat treatment on microstructural evolution and mechanical properties, including hardness, tensile strength, elongation, and impact toughness.

# 🎯 Objectives

- Investigate the effect of Zn content on the microstructure of binary Al-Zn alloys.
- Study the influence of solid-solution heat treatment on microstructural evolution.
- Analyze Zn segregation and phase distribution in as-cast and heat-treated conditions.
- Characterize the alloys using XRD, Optical Microscopy, SEM, TEM, and EDS.
- Evaluate Vickers microhardness, tensile properties, and Charpy impact toughness.
- Establish relationships between composition, processing, microstructure, and mechanical properties.

# 🧱 Materials Studied

Three binary Al-Zn alloy compositions were investigated:

- Al-20Zn
- Al-35Zn
- Al-49Zn

The alloys were fabricated through casting and subsequently subjected to solid-solution heat treatment.
